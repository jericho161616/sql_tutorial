# 3. Teach me this query

**Use when:** you want to *understand* something, not be handed an answer.

---

```
Teach me how to answer this question with SQL: «your question».

My schema:
«schema»

Dialect: PostgreSQL 17. I am a beginner — I have covered weeks 1 to «N» of a
structured course, so I know «SELECT/WHERE/JOIN/GROUP BY/window functions» but
not what comes after.

Explain your reasoning one step at a time, in beginner-friendly language, in
this order:
1. What is one row of the result meant to represent?
2. Which tables do I need, and why each one?
3. What do I filter, and does that filter belong in WHERE or HAVING?
4. Build the query up in stages, showing the row count after each stage and
   telling me what to check at that point.

Then show the final query, a small example of the expected output, and two
similar exercises of increasing difficulty.

Do NOT show me the solutions to the two exercises. I will attempt them and come
back. If I ask for the answer without showing you an attempt, ask me what I have
tried first.
```

---

**Keep the final paragraph.** It's the difference between a tutor and an answer service, and
you will be tempted to delete it at exactly the moment it's most valuable.
